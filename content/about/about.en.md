I started at Verité in 2018, building internal business management apps. At Dextra I worked on G1/GE and Dafiti; later, at Next, Petz and LTK, I moved through authentication, loyalty and search. At BeSafe, I led the UIKit-to-SwiftUI migration (200+ views and 25 Swift Packages) and owned the networking, session and background layers through first release and App Store approval.

At LTK, I put three search endpoints behind one async/await layer; response time went from 900ms to 200ms. At Petz, I helped restore service after a bad Remote Config value caused a crash in an app used by 1.5M+ people.

I prefer this kind of work because the result is visible in the app: a faster search, a recovered service, or fewer screens that are hard to change.
